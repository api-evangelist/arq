# Arq

Arq (formerly DolarApp) is a Latin American cross-border fintech app that gives travelers, investors, and professionals digital dollar and digital euro accounts at real market rates without hidden fees. The mobile-first platform offers currency conversion, a borderless payment card, global accounts, bill payment, PIX transfers, stock and ETF investing, and high-yield Trade and Save accounts.

Arq is a consumer mobile product and does not publish a public API or developer portal. This profile captures its verified public identity and web surface.
